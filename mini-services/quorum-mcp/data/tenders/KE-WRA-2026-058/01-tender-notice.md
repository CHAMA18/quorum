# TENDER NOTICE — KE-WRA-2026-058

**Buyer:** Water Resources Authority
**Reference:** KE-WRA-2026-058
**Subject:** Supply and commissioning of solar submersible borehole pumps, lots 1–3
**Budget ceiling:** KES 8,000,000 (VAT inclusive)
**Closing:** 20 March 2027, 10:00 EAT · Open national tender, two-envelope

## 1. Specifications — the Supplier SHALL:

- The supplier SHALL deliver solar submersible pumps of at least 2 HP with a minimum flow of 3 m³/hour at 60 m head.
- The supplier SHALL supply a controller and inverter per pump.
- The supplier SHALL survey each borehole and carry out a flow test before installation.
- The supplier SHALL provide a five-year manufacturer warranty on the pump.
- Bidders must declare any deviation from these specifications in the specifications matrix.

## 2. Eligibility checklist

- [REQUIRED] Pump test certificate
- [REQUIRED] Manufacturer warranty letter
- [REQUIRED] Tax compliance certificate
- [REQUIRED] Water Resources Authority registration

Scored 60% technical, 40% price. Bids above the budget ceiling are non-responsive.
