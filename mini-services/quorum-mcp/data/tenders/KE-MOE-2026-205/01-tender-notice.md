# TENDER NOTICE — KE-MOE-2026-205

**Buyer:** Ministry of Education · State Department for Basic Education
**Reference:** KE-MOE-2026-205
**Subject:** Supply and installation of student desks and physics laboratory kits — 40 secondary schools
**Budget ceiling:** KES 90,000,000 (VAT inclusive)
**Closing:** 3 March 2027, 10:00 EAT · Open national tender, single envelope

## 1. Specifications — the Supplier SHALL:

- The supplier SHALL deliver double-seat student desks conforming to the KS 1 series standard, 500 per school.

- The supplier SHALL install all units at each school within ninety (90) days of contract signature.
- The supplier SHALL provide a twenty-four (24) month warranty on all equipment.
