# TENDER NOTICE

**Reference:** KE-MSA-2026-090
**Subject:** Supply & delivery of water-quality laboratory kits and reagents — coast region
**Budget ceiling:** KES 240,000,000 (VAT inclusive)
**Closing:** 09 February 2027, 10:00 EAT · Open tender, OCDS release 1.1

## 1. Specifications — the Supplier SHALL:

- The supplier SHALL deliver portable water-testing kits conforming to WHO field-testing guidance in Annex B.
- The supplier SHALL supply reagent refill packs with a minimum shelf life of eighteen (18) months on delivery.
- The supplier SHALL commission each laboratory kit within thirty (30) days of delivery and train at least four (4) county laboratory technicians per depot.
- The supplier SHALL calibrate every incubator against a traceable reference standard before handover.
- The supplier SHALL state the country of manufacture for every line item.
- Bidders must hold a valid tax compliance certificate at submission.
- Bidders must hold current ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation for the assays quoted.

## 2. Eligibility checklist

- [REQUIRED] Manufacturer authorization letter
- [REQUIRED] Bid security or bid-securing declaration
- [REQUIRED] Tax compliance certificate
- [REQUIRED] ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation certificate

## 3. Evaluation

Prices evaluated on total cost of ownership over three years. Arithmetic errors corrected per clause 34. Bids deviating materially from Annex B are non-responsive. The contract will be signed with the successful bidder's registered entity; the procuring entity's details appear on the signed contract form.
