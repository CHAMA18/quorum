# TENDER NOTICE — RW-UMUCYO-2027-006

**Buyer:** Rwanda Biomedical Centre · Umucyo e-procurement
**Reference:** RW-UMUCYO-2027-006
**Subject:** Community health worker digital kits — solar tablets, diagnostics and connectivity (7,500 kits)
**Budget ceiling:** RWF 9,800,000,000 (VAT inclusive)
**Closing:** 03 March 2027, 09:00 CAT · Framework agreement, open to prequalified suppliers

## 1. Specifications — the Supplier SHALL:

- The supplier SHALL deliver solar tablets rated for a minimum of 8 hours continuous field use per charge.
- The supplier SHALL preload the national community-health application and verify boot integrity on every unit.
- The supplier SHALL supply rapid diagnostic test (RDT) cassettes WHO-prequalified for malaria and pneumonia.
- The supplier SHALL enable remote device management with firmware updates signed by the buyer.
- The supplier SHALL replace defective units within ten (10) working days under the framework.
- Suppliers must maintain a repair depot in-country with certified technicians.
- Bidders must guarantee connectivity bundles (SMS + data) for three (3) years.
- Bidders must declare the country of final assembly for all hardware.

## 2. Eligibility checklist

- [REQUIRED] In-country repair depot certificate
- [REQUIRED] WHO prequalification certificates for RDTs
- [REQUIRED] Connectivity bundle guarantee (3 years)
- [REQUIRED] Tax clearance (RRA) certificate

## 3. Evaluation

Framework awarded per lot: tablets, RDTs, connectivity. Evaluated on unit price, support load and five-year connectivity cost. Devices without signed firmware are non-responsive.
