# TENDER NOTICE — KE-KCS-2026-042

**Buyer:** Kenya Customs Service · National Treasury
**Reference:** KE-KCS-2026-042
**Subject:** Supply, installation and support of container inspection scanners (drive-through X-ray) — 3 ports
**Budget ceiling:** KES 1,860,000,000 (VAT inclusive)
**Closing:** 14 February 2027, 10:00 EAT · Open international tender, two-envelope

## 1. Specifications — the Supplier SHALL:

- The supplier SHALL deliver drive-through X-ray scanners with imaging throughput of at least 25 containers/hour per site.
- The supplier SHALL complete installation and radiation-safety certification at each port within one hundred and twenty (120) days of contract signature.
- The supplier SHALL integrate scanner imagery with the existing customs risk-management system via documented API.
- The supplier SHALL train at least forty (40) customs officers as operators and fifteen (15) as image analysts.
- The supplier SHALL provide on-site spare parts for two (2) years and comprehensive maintenance for five (5) years.
- The supplier SHALL obtain type approval from the Radiation Protection Board before energising any unit.
- Bidders must supply reference installations of the same scanner model operating in at least two (2) customs administrations.
- Bidders must disclose total ionising dose per inspected container and the radiation-safety dossier.

## 2. Eligibility checklist

- [REQUIRED] Manufacturer authorization letter
- [REQUIRED] Reference installation letters (2 customs administrations)
- [REQUIRED] Radiation Protection Board type-approval dossier
- [REQUIRED] Original equipment manufacturers statement
- [REQUIRED] Tax compliance certificate

## 3. Evaluation

Scored 40% technical, 30% commercial, 30% five-year support cost of ownership. Units without RPB type approval are ineligible. Throughput guarantees below 25 containers/hour are non-responsive.
