# TENDER NOTICE — KE-MOH-2026-117

**Buyer:** Ministry of Health · Kenya
**Reference:** KE-MOH-2026-117
**Subject:** Supply & delivery of essential medicines tracer list (48 items) to Level 4–6 facilities — 12 counties
**Budget ceiling:** KES 624,800,000 (VAT inclusive)
**Closing:** 30 January 2027, 10:00 EAT · Open tender, national, two-envelope

## 1. Specifications — the Supplier SHALL:

- The supplier SHALL supply only medicines prequalified by the WHO or registered by the PPB.
- The supplier SHALL deliver each tracer item to the county medical stores within forty-five (45) days of contract signature.
- The supplier SHALL assign a shelf life of not less than twenty-four (24) months at delivery for every batch.
- The supplier SHALL transport cold-chain items at 2–8 °C with validated data loggers on every shipment.
- The supplier SHALL report any recall or quality defect to the buyer within twenty-four (24) hours.
- Suppliers must hold a valid PPB wholesaler licence for the contract period.
- Bidders must submit a certificate of analysis for every batch offered.
- Bidders must declare any anti-dumping or subsidy arrangements affecting quoted prices.

## 2. Eligibility checklist

- [REQUIRED] PPB wholesaler licence
- [REQUIRED] WHO prequalification or PPB registration certificates
- [REQUIRED] Certificate of analysis samples
- [REQUIRED] Cold-chain capability report
- [REQUIRED] Tax compliance certificate

## 3. Evaluation

Medicines scored on compliance, lead time and five-year landed cost. Batches shorter than 24 months shelf life are non-responsive. Cooling failures on delivery terminate the lot.
